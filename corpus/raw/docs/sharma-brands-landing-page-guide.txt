# Sharma Brands Landing Page Guide

*A section-by-section breakdown of a high-converting DTC landing page. Each section below shows the example brand used in the mockup, followed by the annotation callouts from the original deck.*

---

## 01. HERO

**Example brand:** JUDY (emergency kits)

Mockup copy:
- Sticky bar (top): "Get 100% off →"
- Headline: **EMERGENCY KITS DESIGNED BY EXPERTS**
- Subhead: Get our best-selling kit for $180, or try **The Ready System** and save **$100 off**.
- Reviews: ★★★★★ 5.0/5
- CTA: **Shop now →**

Annotations:
- **Sticky bar at top should have offer.**
- **Big, bold headline about your brand.**
- **Badge** can be an accolade (BEST SELLER, Oprah feature), or x% OFF.
- **Subhead** should communicate offer in a clear, concise manner.
- If you have good reviews, **call it out here**. Bonus points if you can say "over x amount of 5 star reviews."
- **Clear CTA.** If you're running an offer, play around with your CTA: "Get 25% off now!" / "Save now" / "Get $100 Off."
- **Image** should be what is being offered.

---

## 02. WHY

**Example brand:** Lalo (baby/kids products)

Mockup copy:
- Heading: **Why Lalo?** ("Here is where you simply tell your customers why they need to try your brand/product.")
- Value prop 1 — **Safety:** All of our products exceed required safety standards, and we never use harmful chemicals or substances.
- Value prop 2 — **Design:** With minimalist yet innovative designs, our products are sleek in style and easy to clean.
- Value prop 3 — **Value:** We built the first ever 2-in-1 high chair and play chair, so that you get twice the value for an affordable price.

Annotations:
- If you can, **use icons!** They're much easier for the consumer to digest.
- If icons don't work for you, write a concise paragraph, bullet points, etc. to get your point across.
- Try to **avoid fluff** and focus strictly on the value props.
- Keep your value props on 1-2 lines and easy to read.
- Briefly describe the value prop.

## 03. BRAG BAR

**Example brand:** ceramic cookware

Intro note: "Pick shorter quotes that highlight your brand in the best way. Include logos for social proof. Brag bars can look like either:"

Layout A — three press quotes side by side:
- **POPSUGAR:** Nothing. Ever. Sticks. The reviewer used the cookware for over a month and never had to scrape a single item off its surface.
- **Forbes:** The sets come in a range of hues from sage green to navy, and include magnetic pan racks and a canvas lid holder for easy storage.
- **Business Insider:** Beyond the starter pots and pans, the set adds magnetic pan racks and a canvas lid holder.

Layout B — single large quote with logo:
- **FOOD & WINE:** The ceramic-coated cookware is Teflon-free, nonstick, easy to clean, oven-safe, and works with every type of stovetop.

## 04. SHOP

**Example brand:** JUDY — The Ready System

Mockup copy:
- Section header: **THE READY SYSTEM** — Designed by experts to protect your family
- Product name: **THE READY SYSTEM** ★★★★★ 5.0/5
- **THE SAFE — Your At-Home Kit:** Everything needed for 72 hours of survival, including KN95 masks, emergency food and water, cell-phone battery packs, hand-crank radio, and 100+ more necessities.
- **THE MOVER MAX — For Evacuations:** A puncture-resistant and water-proof bag holding a hand-crank radio, emergency food and water, a multi-use tool, water-proof matches, and 75+ items.
- **THE STARTER — For On-The-Go Daily Use:** 14 unique items including a KN95 mask, first-aid kit, poncho, rescue blanket, and more.
- Discount code callout: Use code SYSTEM100 for $100 off today.
- Price: ~~$490~~ **$390** + Free Shipping
- CTA: **Add To Cart & Save $100 →**
- Financing: Starting at $43/month with Affirm.

Annotations:
- Header: "Shop The Ready System" / "Shop Now"… just reiterate what the product is and a relevant subheading.
- **Clearly list product name**, include reviews if possible.
- **Clearly describe** what the consumer will be receiving.
- **Clearly describe how to apply discount** if not auto-applied (this will save you lots of CX inquiries).
- **Boldly list price**; if discounted, show old price compared to new.
- **Klarna / Afterpay etc.** call out.
- **CTA.**
- **Show multiple views** of the product, highlight any deals or call outs.

**Example brand:** JUDY

Mockup copy:
- Heading: **WHAT FAMILIES ARE SAYING** — See why everyone prepares with JUDY.
- **THE MOVER MAX:** "I am in love! There is plenty of room for me to put our fireproof/waterproof documents folder and pet supplies in the bags as well!" — ★★★★★ Beth M.
- **THE MOVER:** "It really is an amazing product! Even my husband who is in the military said everything was very good quality!" — ★★★★★ Amy C.
- **THE SAFE:** "I'm very pleased with and impressed with my kit. Everything is so organized and thorough, I could never put all of this together on my own." — ★★★★★ Stacy M.
- CTA: **Shop Our Kits →**

Annotations:
- **Get creative** with titling your reviews section.
- **List name of product** being reviewed.
- **Include rating** (should be 4.5+) and name.
- **CTA** to PDP or Collections page.

## 06. HOW IT WORKS / OTHER VALUE PROPS

**Example brands:** Poo-Pourri (how it works), a hydration brand (value props)

Top bar icons: Free Shipping (on orders $90+) · Free Returns · 30-Day Trial

"How it works" steps:
1. **SPRITZ** — Spritz the bowl before you go to create a barrier on the water's surface that traps #2 odor below.
2. **POO** — Proceed with your business as usual. Anyone will smell a refreshing blend of natural essential oils.
3. **SNIFF** — You and your potty guests can leave the toilet smelling better than you found it.

"Proper Hydration Can Bring" (icon row): Vibrant Skin · Quicker Post-Workout Recovery · Increased Focus · Amplified Energy · Improved Mood

Annotations:
- **Icons are great here too.**
- If your product has specific steps, highlight them here.
- If you have more value props than what's in the WHY section, highlight them here.
- Anything crucial that you want to get across to your consumer.

## 08. COMPARISON CHART

**Example brand:** JUDY

Intro note: "Here is where you compare your brand to either 1) specific competitors or 2) general items."

Mockup table — **JUDY VS OTHERS** ("Here's how JUDY compares to other kits."):

| | JUDY | Store-Bought Emergency Kit | Homemade Kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 72 hours of self-sufficiency | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Hand-Picked by Disaster Experts | ✓ | ✕ | ✕ |
| Digitally-Connected & Emergency SMS Alerts | ✓ | ✕ | ✕ |
| Disaster Guides & Planning Documents | ✓ | ✕ | ✕ |
| Pre-Assembled | ✓ | ✓ | ✕ |

CTA: **Shop Now →**

Annotations:
- **Make sure your brand is first**, its logo is on top, and the check marks are different from the rest of the chart.
- **Make sure your product can check off every benefit you list.**
- **Add a SHOP NOW CTA** in case this is where you convince them to buy.
