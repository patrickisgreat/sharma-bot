William Sonoma operates 530 stores across the United States, with an average store size of 11,250 square feet and 21,000 total employees, of which 12,500 are full-time. The company does 8.7 billion in revenue, with 66% coming from e-commerce and 34% from retail stores. Cost of goods sold makes up 58% of revenue at 5 billion dollars, while SG&A is 2.1 billion at 25% of revenue, leaving 1.5 billion in EBITDA. The company has an 8 billion dollar market cap.

William Sonoma actually operates three brands. Pottery Barn does 3.5 billion in revenue with 188 stores. West Elm does 2.3 billion with 122 stores. William Sonoma itself does 1.3 billion with 165 stores. Most of the company's business represents a new chapter in someone's life—the first visit is exciting, but by the third visit, customers often just want to complete their purchase and leave.

Interestingly, West Elm has moved to digital price tags in stores, which raises questions about dynamic pricing. These appear to be e-ink displays similar to Kindle devices, suggesting prices may change throughout the day based on inventory levels and demand.

Singles Day in China did 84 billion in sales last year, flat from 2021. The event has a sophisticated structure where consumers can pre-add items to cart on October 24th, companies can adjust offers from November 1st through 3rd based on that data, and then the actual shopping day is November 11th. Even with restrictions on live streaming from government pressure, Singles Day generated a billion dollars in live stream sales in just 14 hours.

Prime Day last year was two days and generated 12 billion in e-commerce sales, up a billion dollars from the previous year, with 300 million items purchased and almost 2 billion in savings. Black Friday, by comparison, only did 9.2 billion in e-commerce sales, making Prime Day bigger in the e-commerce space.

Shopify launched Shop Day on June 2nd as their version of a major shopping event. The Shop app, which started as an order tracking tool called Arrive, has been rebranded and repositioned as a marketplace and customer acquisition tool. During Shop Day, Shopify distributed millions of dollars in Shop Cash credits. The Shop campaigns feature allows advertisers to set a target CAC and use multipliers on existing Shop Cash balances, though the relationship between bid amount and multiplier can be confusing.

When it comes to daily metrics for e-commerce brands, most dashboards track total ad revenue, ad spend, items sold, shipping costs, revenue per session, AOV, transactions, and platform-specific spend. However, conversion rate is a metric many operators don't prioritize, instead focusing on CAC and ensuring efficient customer acquisition.

Other important daily metrics include inventory levels at fulfillment centers and comparing Stripe versus PayPal payment methods. PayPal usage can indicate lower trust in a brand, as customers typically use it for buyer protection. As brands build trust through reviews, testimonials, and social proof, PayPal transactions typically decrease relative to Stripe transactions.

On a weekly basis, operators look at ingredient or component inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for upcoming production. On a quarterly basis, metrics like email signup form opt-in rate, welcome flow revenue, and multi-purchase thresholds become important. Many brands focus on getting customers to a specific number of purchases, understanding that reaching this threshold indicates high lifetime value potential.

Shopify's new one-page checkout option is gaining adoption among brands, though some implementations still have redundancies. For example, asking for name on card and then separately asking for billing name and last name is redundant—these should be the same field. Similarly, most billing information beyond zip code is unnecessary for credit card processing.

Post-purchase surveys can be valuable for understanding why customers chose to buy and where they discovered the brand, but this real estate competes with post-purchase upsells and phone number capture for text message marketing. Rather than placing surveys on the thank you page, sending them via email an hour after purchase preserves checkout real estate for revenue-generating elements like upsells or subscription captures.

The period between purchase and package delivery represents significant untapped opportunity. Brands can send multiple emails during this window including founder videos, product usage instructions, post-purchase upsells, and surveys. A particularly effective tactic is offering complementary products shortly after purchase—for instance, suggesting a body wash in the same scent to a customer who just bought deodorant.
