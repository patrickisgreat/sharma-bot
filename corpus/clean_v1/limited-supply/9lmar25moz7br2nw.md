What were the lessons from Greats that led to the checklist to start Jolie?

Fashion is very inefficient. It's really hard to build a great fashion business. There are all these challenges: sizing, fit, seasonal changes. Your measurements might make you a medium in one brand and a large in another. Sizing is interpreted by people based on how they like things to fit, not based on universal measurements.

Fashion is hard because you have to change everything every 90 days. Think about how fashion brands are designing fall 25, maybe even spring 26, when we're recording this. They're trying to predict trends months and months out. Nobody's a fortune teller. Trends move ultra fast. By the time products get there, what they thought might be working didn't.

That led me to thinking: what type of business can you have ultimate efficiency around? And particularly for e-commerce. So I created a framework.

The first part was sizing—something that's one size fits all. The second part was vanity, because vanity really drives what we buy and when.
