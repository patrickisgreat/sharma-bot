When you're spending money driving traffic to your site, the reality is that 98% of that traffic remains anonymous. Once those visitors leave, it's nearly impossible to find them again. The fundamental challenge every brand faces is converting these anonymous visitors into customers.

The traditional funnel concept worked well for billboards, TV, radio, and newspapers, but today it's best to think in terms of upper funnel and lower funnel. Upper funnel is about driving awareness and creating brand affinity—what you might call clout or coolness. You want people to discover you. Lower funnel is about driving conversion through platforms like Facebook and TikTok ads. The middle of the funnel is essentially good customer experiences across platforms where prospects do their own research.
